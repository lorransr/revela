# Landing page

Mode: Persuade. Audience: families who want their photos printed as a book without uploading them (weddings, newborns, shoots, trips). Action: copy the `make` command or open the GitHub repo. Proof: the real privacy facts from README, a labeled synthetic processing slip, stock spreads. Constraints: self-hosted fonts and images, EN default and PT by browser, Unsplash photos credited.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the envelope a photo lab hands back: your prints, sealed, yours. It refuses the photobook-site hero (headline, family collage, "Create your book" button, three benefit cards).

OWN-WORLD: Drenched envelope yellow ground, printed in lab red and press black only; the back panel a deeper yellow. Order form in white paper with ruled fields and square tick boxes ticked by a drawn red X. Perforated claim stub with punch holes. Prints with white borders. Archivo 900 for the lab mark, Archivo Narrow caps for form labels. No cream, no serif.

STORY: A family sees their kind of photos turned into a layflat book, believes nothing leaves their computer, fills the order form and copies the command, or opens GitHub.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top strip is the open envelope mouth: deeper yellow, three prints peek out, nav at right. The front panel covers their lower half: "revela" at display scale, the headline in condensed caps, the order form strip (album, format, finish) across the width, and the claim stub below with the live command, Copy (red, primary) and GitHub.

FORM: Photo-lab envelope, my top-ranked grounded candidate (#1 of 7), chosen by the user over the roll. seed:50dac874f83e8db8. Signature interaction: the order form writes the command on the stub. Motion: on scroll the prints rise out of the envelope.

ADAPTATION: on phones the claim stub moves under the album field, so Copy stays in the first 844px; size and finish follow it.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
